# ICLR 2027 submission checklist

- [ ] Keep the review PDF anonymous; no names, affiliations, acknowledgments, personal repository URLs, or identifying metadata.
- [ ] Confirm every author's OpenReview profile and author order before the abstract deadline.
- [ ] Re-verify the official ICLR 2027 dates and policy immediately before submission.
- [ ] Keep main text within 9 pages; references and appendices may follow.
- [ ] Review and truthfully finalize the required AI use statement.
- [ ] Review ethics and reproducibility statements.
- [ ] Verify every bibliography entry against a primary source.
- [ ] Re-run the artifact commands from a fresh environment.
- [ ] Add three-seed main results and at least one additional agent benchmark.
- [ ] Add a matched hard-skill test baseline and selected Skill-KL ablation.
- [ ] Do not tune further on the 280-task SpreadsheetBench test split.
- [ ] Inspect the final PDF for line overflow, clipped plots, font embedding, and identifying PDF metadata.
